#### Overall Impression
The foundation is technically disciplined—clean routes, strict TypeScript, responsive layouts, robust form validation, and strong privacy boundaries. However, the emotional craft, typography, and visual hierarchy fall flat. It feels like an administrative database portal rather than the digital home of cinema craft. Elevating typography, replacing the generic card-stack layouts with cinematic editorial compositions, and streamlining cognitive load in the casting workflow represent the single biggest opportunity.

#### What's Working
1. **Defensive Validation & Error Prevention**: Comprehensive client-side schema enforcement via Zod, strict date boundary checks (no past deadlines, max 365 days), file MIME/size pre-checks before upload, and accessible keyboard-focused confirmation modals on destructive actions.
2. **URL-Synchronized State & Navigation**: Deep, bookmarkable URL state across search parameters, pagination, casting filters, and applicant folder selections. Users can refresh or share active views without state desynchronization.
3. **Rigorous Privacy & Role Protection**: Public profile routes strictly strip non-public scalars (phone, email, internal IDs), casting calls strictly guard role eligibility upfront, and shortlist folders preserve confidentiality for hiring producers.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Category-Interchangeable SaaS Visual Identity & Neglected Editorial Typography**
  - **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md commits to an editorial, cinematic visual tone. The current design uses generic SaaS blue/indigo hues, cold zinc backgrounds, and misses out on `Source_Serif_4` (which sits unused in layout). It fails to project cultural weight or artistic prestige.
  - **Fix**: Re-theme globals.css with a warm deep charcoal base, warm ivory card surfaces, and subtle muted gold accents (`oklch(0.78 0.09 75)`). Enforce `font-serif` on page headings, actor names, and casting titles.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset` or `/impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Public Landing Page Cluttered with Internal DevOps Health Status**
  - **Why it matters**: Displaying an "API and database reachable" health card with a glowing pulse dot directly below the hero on page.tsx destroys immersion and signals an unfinished prototype rather than a production platform.
  - **Fix**: Remove the internal health card from the public homepage. Replace it with an editorial showcase of open featured casting calls and a talent craft spotlight.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable shape`

- **[P1] Rigid "Card Stack" Layout Flattens Visual Talent Presentation**
  - **Why it matters**: In ProfileView.tsx, creative media (headshots, video showreels) are confined to small, uniform cards identical to phone number rows. Film casting depends on immediate visual impact.
  - **Fix**: Redesign the profile into a cinematic layout: a hero portrait frame for headshots, a widescreen video screening slate for showreels with playback meta, and a filmography slate for credits.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P2] High Cognitive Load & Missing Context in Applicant Review**
  - **Why it matters**: In `/casting/[id]/applicants`, directors reviewing dozens of candidates must mentally recall the role's age range, languages, and character brief because the role details are absent from the applicant screen. Additionally, rendering up to 20 folder toggle pill buttons directly on every candidate card creates visual chaos.
  - **Fix**: Add a sticky or collapsible "Role Brief" drawer/sidebar. Consolidate folder pills into a streamlined "Organize..." popover, and add keyboard shortcuts (J/K to advance, S to shortlist, R to reject).
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P2] Silent Unsaved Changes Risk in Multi-Section Profile Editor**
  - **Why it matters**: In `/profile/edit`, 6 sections (Details, Skills, Experience, Portfolio, CV, Photo) save independently. Editing a bio and navigating away or uploading a photo before clicking "Save details" silently discards the user's input.
  - **Fix**: Implement an unsaved dirty-state prompt on navigation and clear visual indicators when scalar fields have pending edits.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`
